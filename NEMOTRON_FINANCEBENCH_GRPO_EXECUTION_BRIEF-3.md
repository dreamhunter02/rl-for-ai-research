## Page 2 — Reward, controlled experiments and deadline

**Freeze the evaluator before final comparisons.** Baseline reward: `R = F × A × (0.5 + 0.5G)`. F = accepted terminal submission; A = verified correctness; G = validated citation/derivation support. Numeric A is binary within declared tolerance; multipart credit follows explicit subclaims, never generic 2% closeness. Text A follows verified facts and contradiction checks. Missing/invalid finish receives zero task reward; log ungated answer quality separately. No finish bonus or repetition penalty. Use zero terminal parse reward with error-turn masking initially; infrastructure failures are invalid samples, not incorrect answers. Nonexistent citations veto task reward, but retain separate factual-correctness diagnostics.

**Judge policy:** deterministic numeric decisions take precedence. Judge unresolved semantic facts; ambiguity, insufficient evidence and provider errors remain unresolved. Preserve independently verified subclaim credit; retry/adjudicate. Unresolved training cases require group-level exclusion and coverage logging. At evaluation, never silently remove hard cases: adjudicate when possible, retain denominators and report unresolved counts plus conservative/optimistic bounds. Version prompts and caches.

**Optional reward ablation only:** `R = F × [0.9A(0.5+0.5G) + 0.1Ret]`, with unchanged evaluator and fixed weights. Ret measures visible supporting evidence; gold-page access alone is diagnostic. Inspect reward-bearing all-wrong groups. Provenance requires evidence-linked operands, not merely a calculator call.

**Training plumbing:** pin SDK/cookbook; verify renderer round trips, centered advantages, generated-token masks, sampled log-probabilities and sampler refresh. Expose LR, rank, temperature, budgets, epochs and checkpoint intervals. Implement shuffled epochs/remainders and count real optimizer updates: floor division currently drops questions. Constant-group filtering does not refill batches; log retained groups and skip zero-advantage updates. Record post-update KL. Match corrected train/dev/eval harnesses; remove the evaluator’s hardcoded six-turn limit. Prefer simple logged behavior to new sampling mechanisms before the deadline.
