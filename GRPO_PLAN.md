# Teacher-Guided On-Policy GRPO Plan

Date: 2026-09-26

## Objective

Train Qwen3.5-4B on FinanceBench with on-policy GRPO while using the existing 91 high-quality teacher traces as a secondary reference and reward-validation resource, without SFT warm-starting and without replaying teacher actions as if they were generated by Qwen.

The primary goal is to improve answer correctness and evidence grounding on the untouched 42-question evaluation split. The teacher signal is auxiliary: it must not overpower FinanceBench answer/evidence correctness or collapse exploration into imitation of one teacher trajectory.

## Current starting point

- Model: Qwen3.5-4B.
- Training hardware: NVIDIA GB10 Spark Two.
- Precision: bf16.
- Adapter: LoRA rank 32, alpha 32.
- Target modules: `q_proj`, `k_proj`, `v_proj`, `o_proj`, `gate_proj`, `up_proj`, `down_proj`.
- Context cap: 16,384 tokens.
- Unsloth: `2026.9.11`.
- TRL: `0.24.0`.
- FinanceBench training split: 108 questions.
- Held-out evaluation split: 42 questions.
- Strict teacher traces: 91 unique training questions, selected from 349 candidates and judged for correctness and grounding.
- Missing teacher-reference coverage: 17 of the 108 training questions.

The current Qwen3.5-4B baseline has approximately `0.704` and `0.708` mean answer reward across two 25-question seeds, and `0.469` and `0.516` mean grounded reward. This is a useful starting point, but evidence grounding remains the main weakness.

## Definitions

### On-policy GRPO

Qwen generates fresh trajectories with the current checkpoint. The harness executes the tools, computes rewards, and the same checkpoint supplies the action-token log-probabilities used for the update. After the update, those trajectories are discarded; they are not replayed as future on-policy data.

### Teacher-guided/reference-guided GRPO

The teacher traces are offline references only. They may validate the deterministic reward, identify correct evidence, or provide a narrowly bounded tie-breaker among Qwen trajectories with equal primary reward. They must not be inserted into the rollout group as extra policy trajectories, used as ordinary SFT targets, or treated as a teacher action distribution.

This remains on-policy with respect to Qwen only when every trajectory used in the GRPO update was sampled from the current Qwen checkpoint and its exact action log-probabilities are retained.

## Teacher-trace usage policy

### Phase 1: reward validation, no teacher reward

Use the 91 traces to verify that:

- answer correctness is scored correctly;
- evidence document/page support is recognized;
- contradictory evidence is penalized;
- missing `finish` is kept separate from answer quality;
- the same question does not produce a false reward due to parser mechanics.

This is the safest use and should happen before teacher guidance affects training.

### Phase 2: optional tie-breaking

Only after several pure answer/evidence GRPO runs should teacher information influence the reward. If multiple Qwen trajectories in one group have identical primary answer/evidence reward, a small tie-breaker may prefer the trajectory whose final claim/evidence provenance is more consistent with the matched teacher reference.

The tie-breaker must never overturn a primary reward difference. It should not compare full tool-call strings or force a particular search order.

### Prohibited teacher uses

- Do not replay teacher actions as Qwen rollouts.
- Do not add teacher trajectories to the GRPO group.
- Do not train with ordinary SFT loss in this experiment.
- Do not reward full-trajectory token or embedding similarity.
- Do not impose KL toward one teacher trace; one trace is not a teacher policy distribution.
- Do not reward a particular tool-call order when multiple valid paths exist.

## Training schedule

### Stage 0: exact bridge verification

The minimal bridge proof has already completed one simplified optimizer step, but the production gate requires exact same-policy rollouts:

- Qwen4B itself generates the assistant/tool actions.
- Tool observations are executed by the existing FinanceBench harness.
- Exact assistant action token IDs, masks, and old log-probabilities are retained.
- The same checkpoint computes the update log-probabilities.
- Group-relative advantages are nonzero.
- The adapter changes and saves successfully.

No SGLang-generated trajectories may be used for the final on-policy GRPO result.

### Stage 1: small pure-GRPO pilot

Start without a teacher reward term:

- 20–30 training questions.
- 10 held-out questions for monitoring.
- Group size: 4 generations per question.
- Two sampling seeds.
- Approximately 50–100 optimizer steps.
- Primary reward: answer correctness plus evidence grounding.
- Log every trajectory and every optimizer statistic.

The teacher traces are used only for reward validation in this stage.

### Stage 2: teacher-guided ablation

If Stage 1 is mechanically sound, compare:

- Pure on-policy GRPO.
- The same run with the bounded teacher tie-breaker.

Use identical questions, seeds, model initialization, optimizer settings, and rollout budgets. Keep the teacher term small and report whether it improves held-out grounded reward rather than only training reward.

### Stage 3: controlled scale-up

If the ablation helps without reducing diversity or held-out performance:

- Expand to the 76 questions with teacher references.
- Add the 17 missing unique-question traces only if full 108-question reference coverage is needed.
- Use at most 2–3 passes initially, with fresh rollouts each pass.
- Stop early if held-out grounded reward falls, reward variance collapses, tool-call entropy collapses, or evidence support worsens.

For a stronger multi-trace reference study, generate 2–3 independent traces for 20–30 hard questions. This means approximately 40–90 additional traces, or 57–107 new traces including the 17 uncovered questions. No additional traces are required for the first feasibility pilot.

## Epoch definition

In this online setting, one epoch means one fresh rollout group for each question in the active training set. It does not mean replaying a fixed teacher dataset.

Planned budget:

- First pilot: one pass over 20–30 questions.
- If stable: one or two additional passes over the active set.
- Hard initial cap: three passes before reviewing validation results.
- Early stopping: based on held-out grounded reward, answer reward, evidence support, and diversity diagnostics.

## Trace capture and run reproducibility

Create an immutable directory:

`results/grpo_runs/<run_id>/`

Before training, save:

- Git commit SHA and working-tree diff.
- Model and tokenizer identifiers.
- Unsloth, Transformers, TRL, PyTorch, CUDA, and hardware versions.
- Full training configuration and reward version.
- Random seeds and RNG states.
- Active question split and teacher-trace hashes.
- Environment and tool-schema version.

For every rollout, save:

- Run, step, batch, group, question, and reference IDs.
- Full message history.
- Every assistant tool call, arguments, and call ID.
- Tool observations, truncation metadata, and provenance.
- Evidence documents/pages and calculations.
- Exact assistant action token IDs and action masks.
- Old and new action log-probabilities.
- Finish arguments and termination reason.
- Answer reward, evidence reward, teacher/tie-breaker signal, final reward, group mean/std, and advantage.
- Latency, token counts, errors, and retry metadata.

For every optimizer step, save:

- Policy loss and KL terms.
- Clipped fraction, entropy, advantage statistics, gradient norm, learning rate, and throughput.
- GPU memory and OOM/error information.
- Checkpoint path and adapter checksum.

Use append-only JSONL for trajectories and metrics, checksums for artifacts, and a final `summary.json`. Do not preserve credentials, API keys, tokens, passwords, or connection strings.

## Experiment tracking

Use MLflow as the open-source run tracker for parameters, scalar metrics, checkpoints, and artifacts. Optionally use Langfuse or Phoenix/OpenTelemetry for a UI over agent trajectories and tool spans.

The canonical source of truth remains the local JSONL trajectory and metric artifacts because generic trackers do not fully represent our multi-turn FinanceBench evidence state.

## Go/no-go gates

Proceed only if:

- The same local checkpoint generated and scored the logged action tokens.
- Tool calls and observations round-trip correctly.
- At least 80% of sampled actions are syntactically valid.
- Finish rate is at least 60% in the exact local-policy pilot.
- At least one group has nonzero reward variance, preferably most groups.
- Gradients and adapter changes are nonzero.
- Empty answers remain zero-reward.
- Teacher guidance never overturns a primary answer/evidence reward difference.
- Held-out grounded reward does not decline materially.
- Search volume, evidence citation, and tool diversity do not collapse.

Do not claim model improvement from a toy run, a reward-calibration audit, or a bridge-only optimizer step. The improvement claim requires comparison with the untouched base checkpoint on the 42-question evaluation split and complete reproducibility artifacts.
